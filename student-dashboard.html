📖 Library Rules

  1. Library mein shor na karein.
  2. Library ko clean aur saaf rakhein.
  3. Dusre students ki padhai disturb na karein.
  4. Kisi ki book, pen ya personal item use karne se pehle permission lein.
  5. Library ki books aur furniture ko damage na karein.
  6. Mobile phone ko silent mode par rakhein.
  7. Apni seat par unnecessary saman na rakhein.
  8. Library staff ke instructions follow karein.
  9. Kachra dustbin mein hi daalein.
  10. Library mein discipline aur peaceful environment maintain karein.
