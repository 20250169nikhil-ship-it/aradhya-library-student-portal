PLEASE FOLLOW

Library Rules

  1. Maintain silence inside the library.
  2. Do not create unnecessary noise.
  3. Keep the library clean.
  4. Do not use another member's belongings without permission.
  5. Do not damage library books, furniture or other property.
  6. Keep your mobile phone on silent mode.
  7. Do not disturb other members while they are studying.
  8. Use only your assigned seat.
  9. Do not write, tear or damage library books.
  10. Pay your library fees on time.
  11. Take care of your personal belongings.
  12. Report any problem to the administration.
  13. Follow library opening and closing timings.
  14. Follow instructions given by the library administration.
  15. Respect other members and maintain discipline.
