Library Rules

  1. Maintain silence inside the library.
  2. Keep the library clean.
  3. Use only your assigned seat.
  4. Keep your mobile phone on silent mode.
  5. Do not disturb other members.
  6. Do not damage library property.
  7. Pay library fees on time.
  8. Take care of personal belongings.
  9. Report problems to administration.
  10. Respect other members and maintain discipline.
